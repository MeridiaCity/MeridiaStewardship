# Meridia Stewardship LLC Operating Agreement

v0.0.0

## 1) Formation

1.1 **Purpose.** Meridia Stewardship LLC (the **Stewardship**) is a manager-managed limited liability company formed under the laws of the State of Texas for the purpose of administering the governance of a privately operated town in coordination with Meridia LLC.

1.2 **Agreement term.** This operating agreement will be effective as of the date that it is formed by Meridia LLC. The Stewardship will continue perpetually except as provided by this operating agreement.

1.3 The *Stewardship's constitution* is the primary document that defines the governance structure of the Stewardship. This document will initially be version 1.0.0 of the Meridia Constitution with hash (INSERT HERE) and will change to a new current version when the previous version was changed based on valid amendments to that version using the rules of amendment of the previous version. Anything in the most current version of this constitution will be binding on the Stewardship and its Signatories, with this operating agreement taking precedence in the case of any contradictions.

1.4 **Corporate tax treatment**. The Stewardship will elect to be taxed as a corporation.

1.5 **Membership**. Any person who has agreed to this operating agreement who owns membership units is considered a Member of Meridia Stewardship LLC and is admitted as a Stewardship Member upon validly receiving ownership over said units. Any person who no longer has any membership units is removed as a Stewardship Member as of the date the number of units of those classes they own becomes 0. 

1.6 **Term definitions.**

- 1.6.1 *Freestead Area* - The land governed by the Stewardship's constitution.
- 1.6.2 *Signatory* - Anyone who has agreed to the Stewardship’s operating agreement and therefore its corresponding constitution, and whose status as a signatory has not been revoked by an action specified in the Stewardship’s constitution.
- 1.6.3 *Stewardship Member* - Anyone who owns any of the Stewardship’s voting member units.
- 1.6.4 *Citizen* - Anyone who has successfully gone through the citizenship process and whose citizenship status has not been revoked.
- 1.6.5 *Meridia Resident* - Citizens who have been inside the *Freestead Area* during at least 180 days of the last 365 days.
- 1.6.6 *Land Holder* - Citizens who hold land in Meridia either by holding a deed to a property within the Freestead Area or by holding a long-term lease to a property with a total term longer than 30 years (both considered the land they "hold") where the lessor is either Meridia LLC, one of its subsidiaries, or the Stewardship.

## 2) Ownership

2.1 All classes of member units are dynamically allocated to Land Holders. Each Land Holder will receive 1 unit per dollar-value of the land they hold within the *Freestead Area* (fractional units being allowed) as assessed by the mechanism specified in the Stewardship's Constitution.

The allocation of these units will be reallocated:

- before the first Stewardship action that takes place in the month for which an accounting of resident units is necessary, or
- before liquidation or dissolution of the Stewardship.

#### **2.2) Distributions**

2.2.1 **No Normal Distributions**. The Stewardship does not generally have the power to make distributions.

2.2.2 **Liquidation Distributions.** Upon dissolution of the Stewardship and after servicing higher-priority obligations, any remaining assets are sold and the proceeds distributed to holders of *membership units*.

2.2.3 **Repayment of Excess Distributions.** If a Stewardship Member receives a distribution in excess of the amount to which that Stewardship Member was entitled under this Agreement, the Stewardship Member will repay the excess amount to the Stewardship upon written demand, if such demand was delivered to the Stewardship Member within 3 years of the excess distribution. This obligation applies regardless of whether the Stewardship Member knew at the time of the distribution that the amount was excessive. Any amount not repaid within 30 days after demand will constitute a debt owed by the Stewardship Member to the Stewardship and may be offset against any future distributions or other amounts otherwise payable to that Stewardship Member.

## 3) Decision making

3.1 Official decisions made by the Stewardship will be governed by the Stewardship's constitution. Changes to this operating agreement may be made under the same conditions as amending the Stewardship's Constitution can be.

3.2 Meridia LLC will be given voting rights according to a contract that will be signed between the Stewardship and Meridia LLC.

3.3 Rights to the collection and use of ground rent between Meridia LLC and the Stewardship will be determined by a contract that will be signed between them.

3.4 In any vote conducted under the Stewardship's Constitution, once the vote is closed to all other potential voters, Meridia LLC will have 1 week to submit its votes.

3.5 Meridia Stewardship LLC may not limit land development to only single-family homes, townhouses, and/or duplexes. 

## 4) Contingencies

4.1 **No transferring equity**. Member units cannot be sold or transferred in any way other than through the process of dynamic allocation mentioned elsewhere in this document.

## 5) Management

5.1 The Mayor selected via the Stewardship's Constitution will serve as the manager of the LLC.

#### **5.2) Books and Records.**

5.2.1 The Stewardship's officers must keep complete and accurate books and records of the Stewardship’s affairs, include the following:

- 5.2.1.1 A current list of the legal names and last known addresses of each Stewardship Member.
- 5.2.1.2 A copy of the Stewardship's certificate of formation, current Operating Agreement, and any related amendments.
- 5.2.1.3 Copies of the Stewardship's tax returns, tax related reports, financial statements, and any records necessary for corporate income tax reporting.
- 5.2.1.4 A record of all decisions made by vote of member units.
- 5.2.1.5 A record of all significant Stewardship decisions made by Stewardship officers.
- 5.2.1.6 A complete and accurate accounting of the Stewardship's affairs.
- 5.2.1.7 Records of all financial transactions.
- 5.2.1.8 Each Stewardship Member's membership interests.

5.2.2 These records will be kept in accordance with any consistent method of accounting as selected by the Stewardship's officers.

5.2.3 Access to Stewardship records will be governed by the Stewardship's Constitution and documents that derive therefrom.

5.2.4 Records shall be retained for at least 7 years.

5.2.5 Separate Stewardship Member capital accounts and allocations of taxable income, gains, losses, and deductions shall not be required.

## 6) Disputes

6.1 **Applicable Disputes**. This section applies to any disputes that happen between any of A. the Signatories or B. the Stewardship or its boroughs regarding actions that happened within the Freestead Area or physically interacted with the complainant in the Freestead Area. 

6.2 **Dispute procedure**. The Stewardship's constitution will govern the procedures to resolve disputes.

6.3 **Binding Arbitration.** In an applicable dispute, a complainant may submit the issue to arbitration but may not pursue the issue in a government court. The arbitration shall take place either in the Freestead Area, within 30 miles of the Freestead Area, or virtually. The arbitration decision shall be final and binding, and judgment upon the award may be entered in any court that has jurisdiction. All signatories acknowledge and agree that they are **waiving their rights to a trial** in municipal, county, state, or federal court in favor of having their disputes resolved by binding arbitration. This waiver will apply to as many disputes as the law allows. 

6.4 **Court remedies waived**. To the fullest extent that law allows, Signatories agree to not sue using, or in any other way use a court of a municipal, county, state, national government, or any other higher-level government for events that fall into the category of Applicable Disputes except as described by the Stewardship's Constitution and related documents.

6.5 **Unwaivable court remedies**. The Signatories agree that claims arising from the same facts may be brought in separate proceedings where some claims fall within the jurisdiction of arbitration under this operating agreement and other claims fall within the jurisdiction of government courts. Each Signatory waives any defense of Res Judicata or claim preclusion based on the failure to bring such claims together in one proceeding, regardless of the order in which the proceedings occur. Each Signatory also waives collateral estoppel or issue preclusion arising from a decision in one proceeding, to the extent that it would prevent independent adjudication of claims reserved for a different jurisdiction under this Agreement. If a dispute involves both claims under the jurisdiction of this operating agreement and claims under the jurisdiction of government courts, the parties agree to joint motion to stay the arbitration proceedings until the higher-level court determines the statutory issues.

* *Justification:  The provisions waiving Res Judicata and an agreement to stay the case when there are claims commingled with higher level law are to avoid voiding of Freestead/borough court decisions when they must leave higher level law for higher level courts and make court decisions without considering those higher level laws.*

## 7) Miscellaneous

7.1 **Severability**. For this Agreement, the Stewardship's constitution, and any additional documents that Signatories agree to be bound to via those documents directly or indirectly, if any provision of any of those documents is held unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted by law and modified to the minimum extent necessary for the remainder to remain in full force and effect.
