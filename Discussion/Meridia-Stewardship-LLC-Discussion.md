## 2) Ownership
2.1 Ownership is here mostly to determine where any proceeds go in the unlikely case of liquidation. Dynamic allocation allows us to distribute ownership rights fluidly as the landscape of land holders changes. Specifying a monthly allocation may be less burdensome than instantaneous allocation eg every time someone sells their lease or splits their plot. Allocation before liquidation ensures that a big thing like that requires the allocation be resolved first so there aren't any oddities that do things like cause someone who has sold their land to get a distribution that the person they sold their land to should get. 

#### 2.2) Classes of Member Units:
* Each of these member units is a different voting constituency. Resident citizens get one vote per person, and land holders get votes proportional to their land area and value. The purpose of this is to ensure governing power is only wielded if all 3 of those groups substantially agree.
* Meridia LLC is given voting power so the company can safeguard the process in the early days. This power will diminish over time.
* Not only land holders get a say in the LLC, resident citizens also do, which disqualifies it from being considered an HOA.

#### 2.3) Distributions
* The Stewardship isn't intended to be a for profit company, so it won't do any distributions unless it is dissolved.
* Liquidation distributions is paid out to holders of land value units because those are the landowners who hold the valuable land the liquidation distributions are derived from.
* Repayment clause because otherwise someone who receives a mistaken distribution might not be required to repay.
