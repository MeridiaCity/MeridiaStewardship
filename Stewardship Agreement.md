# Stewardship Agreement

This agreement is made between Meridia LLC and Meridia Stewardship LLC for the purpose of establishing the coordination between them.

## 1) Term definitions

1.1 *The Company* will refer to Meridia LLC.

1.2 *The Stewardship* will refer to Meridia Stewardship LLC.

1.3 *Signatory* - Anyone who has agreed to the Stewardship’s operating agreement and therefore its corresponding constitution, and whose status as a signatory has not been revoked by an action specified in the Stewardship’s constitution.

1.4 *Citizen* - Anyone who has successfully gone through the Stewardship citizenship process and whose citizenship status has not been revoked.

1.5 *Freestead Area* - The land governed by the Stewardship's constitution.

1.6 *Land Holder* - Citizens who hold land in Meridia either by holding a deed to a property within the Freestead Area or by holding a long-term lease to a property with a total term longer than 30 years (both considered the land they "hold").

## 2) Stewardship equity rights

2.1 The Company will allow any of its members to sell any class of member units to the Stewardship.

2.2 If the Stewardship purchases *development units*, they will be converted into *operator units*. 

## 3) Stewardship rights of first refusal

3.1 In the case that the Company decides to liquidate more than 10% of its assets or more than 10% of the land held by Meridia LLC, and any group of Company members does not purchase some of those assets within 40 days, the Stewardship will have a right of first refusal with a time limit of 70 days from the decision to liquidate, or 40 days from an offer on those assets being accepted, whichever is longer. If the proposed consideration includes non-cash consideration that cannot reasonably be matched, the Stewardship may instead provide cash having an equivalent fair market value.

## 4) Meridia LLC voting rights

4.1 The Company will initially be allocated voting rights equal to 50% of the vote weight for each of the three weighting methods in the Freestead council, the Freestead local committees, the council of the first borough, and the local committees when they vote on borough matters. This fraction will be reduced by 5% for each year after the first resident unit is allocated. As clarification, this means that after 2 years, The Company will be allocated 40% of each vote weighting, after 5 years 25%, and after 10 years, zero.

4.2 In any vote weighted by Stewardship member units, once the vote is closed to all other Stewardship Members, The Company will have 1 week to submit its votes.

## 5) Ground rent rights

5.1 **Ground rent fractions.** From time to time, Meridia LLC and the Stewardship will decide what fraction of the land's rental value each wants to collect as a ground rent. The Stewardship may choose a fraction no larger than 0.01 + fractionLandInterest*.99 where fractionLandInterest is the Stewardship's fraction of capital interest in the Company. The Company’s fraction may be no larger than one minus the Stewardship's maximum fraction.

5.2 **Land value evaluation**. The Stewardship and Meridia LLC will each decide a process to determine the land's rental value. Lease contracts entered into by the Company and its subsidiaries out to other parties will specify that the lease holder agrees to accept both Meridia LLC's method of determining the land's rental value it has at the time of lease-signing and the Stewardship's method of determining the land's rental value (which may change in a manner specified in the Stewardship's constitution).

5.3 **Ground rent collection**. The Stewardship will facilitate the collection of the ground rent from all land leased by Meridia LLC and its subsidiaries out to other parties. The Stewardship will retain its chosen fraction of the collected ground rent and use it for its own purposes and release the rest to Meridia LLC. Meridia LLC will retain the right to facilitate collection of its own share of ground rent instead of the Stewardship facilitating the collection of that share if it so chooses.

## 6) Access rights

6.1 The Stewardship's constitution may decide criteria restricting what parties may hold land in the *Freestead Area*. The deed or long-lease for properties must be structured to require the land holder to be a signatory of the operating agreement of Meridia Stewardship LLC, except as defined in 6.2.

6.2 In the case of default of the land holder, the Stewardship's constitution may not prevent land holdership from being transferred to a lender who has lent money to the land holder with the land holdership as collateral. In such a case, the lender may hold it in their custody for any length of time necessary to sell the lease to someone, but may not allow occupancy of the property until it is owned by someone authorized by the rules set forth in the Stewardship's constitution.

6.3 The Company and its subsidiaries retain the right to sell land they own within the Freestead Area outright with no deed restriction tying the property to the Stewardship.
