## Meridia Freestead Governance Repository

This repository contains the constitution, ordinances, and public voting records of the Meridia Freestead. 

### Meridia Documents

* [Meridia Stewardship LLC](Meridia-Stewardship-LLC.md) - Operating agreement for the Meridia Stewardship LLC which will contain the constitutional Freestead structure of Meridia. *See also the [discussion](Discussion/Meridia-Stewardship-LLC-Discussion.md).* 
* [Constitution of Meridia](Constitution-of-Meridia.md) - The constitution of the Freestead which defines the structure of the Freestead governments and their limitations. *See also the [discussion](Discussion/Constitution-of-Meridia-Discussion.md).* 
* [Stewardship Agreement](Stewardship-Agreement.md) - Agreement to make between Meridia LLC, which will own the land, and Meridia Stewardship LLC to establish the relationship between them. *See also the [discussion](Discussion/Stewardship-Agreement-Discussion.md).* 

### Wilsboro Documents

* [Constitution of Wilsboro](Constitution-of-Wilsboro.md) - The constitution of the first Meridia borough. *See also the [discussion](Discussion/Constitution-of-Wilsboro-Discussion.md).*
