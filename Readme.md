## Meridia Freestead Governance Repository

This repository contains the constitution, ordinances, and public voting records of the Meridia Freestead. 

### Meridia Documents

* [Meridia Stewardship LLC](Meridia Stewardship LLC.md) - Operating agreement for the Meridia Stewardship LLC which will contain the constitutional Freestead structure of Meridia.

* [Stewardship Agreement](Stewardship Agreement.md) - Agreement to make between Meridia LLC, which will own the land, and Meridia Stewardship LLC to establish the relationship between them.

* [Constitution of Meridia](Constitution of Meridia.md) - The constitution of the Freestead which defines the structure of the Freestead governments and their limitations. 

  

### Wilsboro Documents

* [Constitution of Wilsboro](Constitution of Wilsboro.md) - The constitution of the first Meridia borough.
